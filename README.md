# Heat Overdrive

Forge 1.20.1 mod inspired by ULTRAKILL 0-E's heat event.

## Commands
- `/heat overdrive start`
- `/heat overdrive stop`
- `/heat overdrive status`

## Behavior
When active, every player in every loaded dimension receives a draining Heat Resistance meter. Water and rain cool players. At zero resistance, overheating deals damage. Nearby terrain can experience controlled rupture effects, small craters, lava leaks, water-to-lava incidents, ice/snow melting, explosion particles/sounds, orange heat tint, and distance-scaled camera shake.

The system is dimension-agnostic and operates in the Overworld, Nether, End, and modded/datapack dimensions.
